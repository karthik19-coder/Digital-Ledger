# AI Assistant Instructions & Constraints

## Business Logic & Calculations
- **NEVER invent business logic.** Every calculation must come directly from explicitly provided specifications.
- ❌ Do NOT decide how profit is calculated.
- ❌ Do NOT invent stock formulas.
- ❌ Do NOT invent dashboard metrics.
- If any calculation or business logic is needed and isn't specified in the prompt, **STOP and ask the user** or wait for the next prompt.
